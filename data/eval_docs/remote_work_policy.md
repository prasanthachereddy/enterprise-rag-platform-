# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing six months of service. Managers approve remote schedules in the HR portal.

## Equipment
The company provides one laptop and one monitor to each remote employee. Equipment must be returned within seven days of leaving the company.

## Working Hours
Remote employees must be available between 10:00 and 16:00 India time on working days. Meetings are not scheduled outside these hours without agreement.
