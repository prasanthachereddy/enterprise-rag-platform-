# Health and Safety Policy

## Fire Safety
Fire drills are held every six months. Employees must use the stairs, never the lifts, during a fire alarm.

## First Aid
Trained first aiders are listed on every floor notice board. The first aid room is on the ground floor next to reception.

## Reporting Hazards
Unsafe conditions must be reported to the facilities team on the same day. Do not use equipment that has been marked as unsafe.
