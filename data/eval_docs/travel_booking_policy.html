Travel Booking Policy

Booking

All flights and trains must be booked through the company travel portal at least seven days before departure. Bookings made outside the portal are not reimbursed.

Hotels

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 6,000 rupees per night in metro cities and 4,000 rupees per night elsewhere.

Cancellations

Cancel unused bookings in the portal as soon as plans change. Cancellation fees caused by late changes may be recovered from the traveller.
