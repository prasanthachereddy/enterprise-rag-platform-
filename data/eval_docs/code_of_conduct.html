Code of Conduct

Respect at Work

Harassment, bullying and discrimination are not tolerated. Complaints can be raised with HR or through the anonymous ethics line.

Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any financial interest in a supplier or competitor to their manager in writing. Disclosure must happen before any business decision involving that company.

Gifts

Gifts worth more than 1,500 rupees from suppliers must be declined or handed to HR. Cash gifts are never accepted.
