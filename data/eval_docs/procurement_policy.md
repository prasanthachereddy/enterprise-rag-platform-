# Procurement Policy

## Purchase Approval
Purchases up to 25,000 rupees need manager approval. Purchases above 25,000 rupees need approval from the finance head.

## Vendor Selection
Purchases above 100,000 rupees require quotations from at least three vendors. The reason for choosing a vendor must be recorded.

## Invoices
Vendor invoices are paid within 45 days of receipt. Invoices without a purchase order number are returned to the vendor.
