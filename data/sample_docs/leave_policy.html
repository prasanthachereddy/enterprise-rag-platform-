Leave Policy

Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave must be requested in the HR portal at least five working days in advance.

Unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried forward to the next calendar year. Carried-forward leave expires on 31 March.

Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than three consecutive days.
